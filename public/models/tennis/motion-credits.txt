Source recordings for subject jarua: jarua_Derecha_18seg.bvh, jarua_VDerecha.bvh and jarua_VReves.bvh. The Tennis-Agent adaptation trimmed and sampled the performances at 50 Hz, converted units, normalized facing and travel, retargeted the rotations to the character and calibrated a fixed racket grip. Court Atlas packages these tracks in glTF, reconstructs the racket release and wrist orientation from the captured hand and elbow paths, blends the stroke with recovery steps and synchronizes a choreographed ball to editorial impact markers. The original dataset records body motion. Ball and racket tracking were authored for this experience.
